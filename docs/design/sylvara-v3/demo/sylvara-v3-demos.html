Controls

W A S D or arrows move · Shift sprint 9 m/s · Space or right-click dash 3 m · 1–9 option · P pause · R restart. On a phone, drag to move and tap to dash.

Assumptions (proposal, Medium): player HP 100, player radius 0.4 m, bullet radius 0.35 m. Stamina 100, dash costs 30, sprint drains 25/s, and it regenerates 20/s after 0.75 s. No iframes and no post-hit immunity: each bullet and each ground hit lands once. Ground warnings are at least 2 s, with an outward-fill countdown inside a fixed coral outline, and turquoise dotted marks show safe gaps. Bots are simple dodgers, there only to show targeting at 2, 4 and 8 players. Hitting zero HP counts a down and refills, so the loop keeps going.
