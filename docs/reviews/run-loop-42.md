## Actionable findings

### P1 — Projectile killing blow invalidates the collection being iterated

`CombatWorld.cs:176-190` fetches `live[i]`, calls `w.DamageBoss(...)` on contact, then calls `live.RemoveAt(i)`. That damage calls `BossEncounter.TakeHit` (`BossEncounter.cs:68-77`), whose killing path calls `Die` and `W.ClearTransient()` (`BossEncounter.cs:84-89`). `CombatWorld.ClearTransient` immediately calls `Projectiles.ClearAll()` (`CombatWorld.cs:80-86, 195`), emptying `live`. Returning to `RemoveAt(i)` throws `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`. A normal lethal Resonant Note or Clockbolt takes this path; no duplicate packets or co-op are needed. The hit callback can also grant Harmony/Ticks after death cleanup.

Reproduce in Unity: halt a practice boss; set it to 1 HP using the existing debug fraction hook; aim a Note/Clockbolt at it and let that projectile deliver the lethal hit. Observe the player log and transition. Also queue multiple projectiles before the kill. Fix by consuming/removing the projectile before dispatching damage and making death/cancellation safe for all active iteration, or by staged deferred mutation with explicit ordering. Keep the existing no-damage-after-boss-death rule; deferring cleanup must not let remaining hazards resolve after a lethal hit. Add a real projectile-kill regression; existing `DebugDefeat()` checks bypass the faulty caller.

**Related path:** `ArcanistKit.cs:101-106` iterates `seals` backwards. If a later-index expired seal kills a boss while an earlier seal remains, `Die -> ClearTransient -> ClearTargetState` clears that list; the next `seals[i]` access can also throw. Ordinary casting usually makes the oldest seal expire first, so this related case is chiefly a checkpoint/restoration or unusual-state regression; do not claim it was observed in normal play. The same dispatch-safety rule should cover it.

### P2 — Dash travels 3.25 m in 13 simulation displacements

`PlayerController.cs:235-239` starts a dash with `dashTick = 12` **and** moves `3/12 = 0.25 m` on that start tick. Each of the next twelve ticks takes the `dashTick > 0` branch, decrements and moves another 0.25 m (`:222-225`). An unobstructed dash therefore travels 13 × 0.25 = **3.25 m**, instead of the README's 3 m over 12 ticks/0.2 s. Finite-stamina escape checks based on the documented distance are measuring a different rule. Count the start tick inside the twelve displacement ticks (or define elapsed/remaining semantics consistently), and verify displacement, cost and recovery at the arena center with no collision.

### P2 — Refreshing a fully strengthened bell keeps multiplying existing echoes

`ChimesingerKit.cs:163` caps `Strength` at two, but multiplies every stored echo by 1.5 on **every** refresh, even when strength was already two. Existing echo damage can therefore grow beyond the documented two strengthening refreshes while the HUD remains `++`. Newly stored echoes use the capped strength (`:175`), making stored echoes disagree depending on refresh history. Two charges plus 6 s recharge and an 8 s bell lifetime allow repeated refreshes to keep the bell alive.

Reproduce: establish a bell and at least one echo; refresh until Strength=2, keep refreshing before expiry, then detonate. Compare with the capped formula. Multiply existing echoes only by the newly granted strength delta, or store base echo amounts and apply the capped multiplier at detonation. Verify the third and fourth refresh leave effective echo strength capped while refreshing lifetime.

### P2 — The advertised 0.15 s buffer does not expire while stunned or dashing

All three kits age `bufferAge` only inside `Step` (`VanguardKit2.cs:93-94`, `ChimesingerKit.cs:91-92`, `ArcanistKit.cs:117-118`). `PlayerController.cs:212-225, 256` invokes only `TickTimersOnly` while stunned/dashing, and those methods omit buffer expiry. A buffered action can consequently activate after a 0.25 s stun or full dash despite a nine-tick TTL. Reproduce by queuing an eligible secondary near the end of another action, applying a hit before it starts, waiting more than nine ticks, and releasing all input: the stale request can fire on recovery. Use an absolute simulation expiry tick; distinguish not-yet-eligible, expired and cancelled requests in U3.

`VanguardKit2.ResetAll` (`:69-75`) and `ChimesingerKit.ResetAll` (`:67-72`) additionally omit clearing the buffer; Arcanist does clear it. Treat that as an incomplete reset invariant. Normal three-second clear transitions usually age out these buffers before the next intro, so this review does **not** assert an observed next-encounter cast. Add reset-fixture coverage instead of relying on the current transition delay to remove stale state.

### P2 — Incoming damage totals include overkill, unlike outgoing damage

`PlayerController.cs:166-170` computes mitigated requested damage, clamps HP to zero, then reports the entire amount. `CombatWorld.cs:135-138` adds it to `DamageTaken` and the recap. Example: 5 HP receiving 30 damage logs 30 taken although only 5 HP was removed. Conversely, `BossEncounter.cs:71` returns damage capped to boss HP. The current UI does not distinguish effective loss from overkill, so the two directions have different metrics. Record requested, mitigated/absorbed, effective and overkill separately; base headline damage taken on effective HP loss and expose overkill explicitly. No change to the attack's authored damage is needed.

### P2 — DPS time bases include post-combat time and do not freeze encounter segments

`RunDirector.cs:230` increments `RunTicks` during Combat **and Cleared and Downed**; `RunHud.cs:184-187` labels it “Time in combat” and uses it for run DPS. A successful four-boss run adds twelve seconds of clear delays. Encounter DPS uses `(World.Tick - SegmentStartTick)` (`RunHud.cs:316`), while `World.Step` increments Tick even outside combat (`CombatWorld.cs:68-72`). Leaving the intermission open keeps decreasing the previous encounter's DPS. Freeze segment end/duration on the terminal combat tick, accumulate active combat ticks for the run, and retain separate elapsed-run time if desired. Validate that waiting at Cleared, Intermission and Results cannot change a completed combat segment's DPS.

## Policies and architecture gaps

- **Health/potions match the newer human policy.** `RunDirector.cs:170-171` refills health for encounters after the first and potions only for the final boss. `PlayerController.cs:137-143` implements that refill; new players begin full. Practice gets a fresh world. Do not reintroduce the obsolete carry-health/no-healing proposal.
- **No global iframes:** hazard resolution checks only Down, same-hazard hit identity, geometry and explicitly authored jump height (`Hazards.cs:122-129`). Distinct hazards may hit in the same tick; this is intentional, and repeated-hit fairness requires encounter validation rather than blanket immunity. Brace and Anchor are specific approved mitigation exceptions.
- **Geometry footprint must be explicit:** `Hazards.cs:41` uses a 0.3 m margin, while collision uses `PlayerController.Radius = 0.45 m` (`PlayerController.cs:79`). The former claims to be the player body radius. Resolve whether these are deliberately separate hurtbox/collision radii before publishing escape fixtures. Telegraphs render nominal dimensions while containment expands them, so “geometry equals telegraph” alone does not explain the boundary tolerance to humans.
- **Step ordering:** players (including their damage) run before boss scheduling, then projectiles, then hazards (`CombatWorld.cs:68-75`). Boss death cancels hazards immediately, so a player kill earlier in a tick prevents their pending resolution. The director checks defeat before boss death *after* World.Step (`RunDirector.cs:238-248`); this supports defeat precedence when both states exist, but does not make all mathematically simultaneous contacts execute. Publish explicit ordering/cancellation semantics instead of asserting universal simultaneous-hit fairness.
- **Bounds/collision:** `Constrain` first clamps player center to 11.5 m and then separates from boss (`CombatWorld.cs:143-159`); shared geometry needs an explicit center-versus-body floor boundary and collision resolution order. Players are corrected before boss movement, so an approaching boss may overlap until the next player step. No party collision or continuous swept collision is demonstrated.
- **Missing U3 authority:** no stable player/session/attempt event identities, immutable command/result protocol, bounded event replay, RNG checkpoint, complete state restoration, explicit roster/reset API, assignment repair or down/revive lifecycle. `AnyStanding` and a players list are useful seams, not co-op implementation. `PrepareForEncounter` does not restore Down to false; reuse for co-op revival would be incorrect. The shipped director creates exactly one actor. Networking remains held.
- **Content limits:** only one implemented difficulty schedule; D1–D6, HP/damage, hit-stun chains, finite-stamina/wall-aware escape and Arcanist ripe-window usability still require relevant encounter/human checks. Boss art stand-ins, no audio, the known Chimesinger export, procedural reactions and continued idle FX while paused remain accurately labelled README limitations.

## Verification and follow-up

This combines an exact-commit source review with an independent diagnostic run inside Unity 6000.6.6f1. The [diagnostic probe](fixtures/FoundationReviewProbe.cs), copied only into the disposable validation project after the baseline build/Play Mode run, reproduced all four requested vulnerabilities: a 3.2500 m dash in 13 displacement ticks (70 stamina remaining), capped bell Strength=2 while an existing 20-damage echo grows to 30 then 45, a 9-tick buffered Bellstrike surviving 15 stunned ticks and starting on recovery, and a projectile killing blow throwing ArgumentOutOfRangeException after the boss dies and clears the projectile list. [Exact report](evidence/review-probe.txt). The probe exited 0 because the expected vulnerabilities were reproduced; this is not a passing gameplay regression suite. The related expired-seal mutation path remains a static finding, not an observed ordinary-play failure.

No archived test result is counted. Independent bootstrap/player/editor evidence is in [U0 validation](u0-validation.md). The baseline `RunAutoTest` covers valuable transitions, reset, clips, icons and sample kit rotations, but its debug kills and controlled short fights leave these normal damage-dispatch bugs and full boss fairness untested.

Required next regressions: lethal Note/Clockbolt with other projectiles pending; expired-seal kill with earlier live seal; exact dash tick/displacement/cost/recovery; third-plus bell refresh at cap; buffer expiry through stun/dash and reset; 5-HP/30-damage overkill; shielded hit and effective heal/overheal; segment freeze and per-actor fatal recap. Run those in the pinned Unity build and, after extraction, the same adopted scene-independent rules in standalone C#.
