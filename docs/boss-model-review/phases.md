# Boss review: fight phases

Concept images, not rigged 3D assets. Thresholds and mechanics are proposed, not implemented or playtested. In-image prose is illustrative and does not override this specification.

| Boss | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aurelion | 100–65%: Perfect Order. Intact armor and aligned halo. Isolated cuts, pins, thrusts and rings establish locked targeting. | 65–30%: Misalignment. Cracked shoulder and independent halo arcs. Pins lead into cuts; rear camping enables Crown Reversal. | 30–0%: Exposed Meridian. Open chest pendulum. Sequential rings lead, after recovery, into repositioning thrusts. |
| Crucivar | 100–65%: Sealed Furnace. Insulated chassis. Isolated hammer, slag, ram and vent attacks. | 65–30%: Casting Heat. Insulation falls away. Slag placement precedes sequential casting strips, preserving a cold route. | 30–0%: Open Pressure. Flywheel guard and shoulder vents open. Rams change casting angles; directional vents pressure close players. |
| Sylvara | 100–65%: Dormant Choir. Closed buds and close-set bells. Individual cones, root lanes, seeds and ordered blooms. | 65–30%: Awakening Bloom. Open flowers and separated bells. Seeds resolve before bloom sequences, requiring planned repositioning. | 30–0%: Hollow Refrain. Split mask and exposed root mechanisms. Root lanes retract before blooms; more repositioning through Briar Procession. |
| Bellkeeper | 100–70%: Last Guardian. Compact rings and closed shell. Introduce sweep, toll, march, chime and one isolated memory repeat. | 70–35%: Broken Hour. Separated shell and offset rings. Short two-pattern combinations and one stored world-space attack footprint. | 35–0%: Final Toll. Fully open chest and spread halo. March, toll, chimes and a memory/sweep pairing when a common safe route exists; 3.5-second final recovery. |

Transitions queue until the active attack finishes, then use a brief readable transformation without damaging the player. Bosses remain targetable; seals, bells and inscriptions persist, with timers continuing normally. Do not stack multiple phase attacks if burst damage crosses thresholds. At most two damaging patterns overlap, only after route validation with real collision, finite stamina and no assumed invulnerability. Avoidance works for melee and ranged characters; retain punish windows rather than uniformly accelerating attacks.
