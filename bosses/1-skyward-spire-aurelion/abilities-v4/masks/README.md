## Format

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 1024 × 1024 px = 30 × 30 m (34.13 px per metre), arena centre at the image centre |
| Colours | **coral `#FF6F5E` = danger**, **warm stone `#D9D2C3` = safe**; outside the play area is transparent |
| Orientation | Game **+z (yaw 0) is up**, +x (yaw 90°) is right; yaw turns clockwise seen from above. In the **gameplay camera** the view is flipped vertically: the camera sits on the +z side, so +z is at the **bottom** of the screen. Flip vertically to match a gameplay screenshot. |
| Shapes | Nominal hazard shapes. The hit test adds a **0.3 m player margin** (`Hazard.Margin`) on top. |
| Arena | Skyward Spire play bound: a circle of **13.9 m** radius, centred on the origin |
