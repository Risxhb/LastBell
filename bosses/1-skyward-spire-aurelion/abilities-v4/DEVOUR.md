# A17 Devour (proposal)

Aurelion v4, every difficulty. It follows Zenith Collapse and is fed by Solar Hunger. It is an original adaptation of Dimensius's Devour, with no copied numbers. The telegraph audit lists it as A17, design-only.

## What it does
- When a Zenith Collapse lands, Aurelion breathes in the light for **2.0 s**. During that breath every standing player is **pulled toward him**.
- The pull grows with each Collapse in the fight, like Solar Hunger: **1.5, then 2.0, then 2.5 m/s** (the cap).
  - Easy: 1.0 m/s with no growth.
  - Hard: same speeds as Medium. Hard is already harder because its Collapse window is shorter.
- Walking is 6 m/s, so walking away always beats the pull, by at least 3.5 m/s. A dash still covers its full distance.
- The pull **deals no damage**. The danger is the Meridian Lances that follow 1.2 s after the Collapse. Their gaps open 5 m out, and the pull drags you back toward the middle, where every beam meets. Standing still is how you get hit.
- It pulls only. It never pushes, so nobody is pinned against the wall, and no hit gets a knockback because of it.

## Where it plays
Devour follows every Zenith Collapse in the plan:
- Phase 1 and Phase 2: Collapse + Lances.
- Phase 3: Sunspots, then Collapse. There, the pull drags you toward the scorch pools nearer the middle.

It never plays in Total Eclipse or Last Light, because neither has a Collapse.

## How it looks
- Gold dust streams from the rim toward his chest along 16 spokes.
- The dust speed matches the pull.
- A **DEVOUR** label shows for the 2.0 s.
- There is no coral ground footprint, because the pull itself never hurts. The coral warnings stay the Collapse ring and the Lances lanes.

## Rules check
- No one-shot.
- No class needed: every class out-walks the pull.
- No immunity.
- Same for 1–4 players. The pull is per player and isn't scaled by party size.

## Open questions
1. Should the pull hit everyone, or only players outside the Collapse's safe disc? The proposal is everyone, so melee feel it too.
2. Are 1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5 m/s right? The alternative is a fixed 2.0 m/s.
3. Should Easy have no Devour at all, instead of the gentle 1.0 m/s?
