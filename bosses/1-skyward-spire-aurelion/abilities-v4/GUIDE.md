# How to beat Aurelion, the Meridian Regent (v4, mythic)

A player's guide to the current v4 fight on every difficulty.

**Proven beatable:** four perfect bots killed him on Hard at full HP in **2:16** (4 of 4 standing). Solo bots killed him on every level with every class: Vanguard (Easy 3:44, Medium 4:38, Hard 4:35), Chimesinger (3:40, 3:34, 3:26) and Arcanist (2:18, 2:40, 2:34); see **Playing solo**. (The video `aurelion-v4-win.mp4` shows an earlier 5:06 version.)

**Numbers:**
- Damage is quoted for Medium. Hard is ×1.3, Easy ×0.6.
- Light hits: 30 (39 on Hard).
- Heavy hits: 50 (65 on Hard).
- You have 100 HP and 5 potions (+40 each, 1 s apart, key F).
- His HP: 10,000 solo, scaled up for a party.

## The five rules that win the fight

1. **Never leave a tower empty.** Blue numbered columns must have someone inside when they land, even solo. An empty tower ends the fight.
2. **Know where to be before the red appears.** Ecliptic Order, SUN/SHADE and Sunspots show their red shape only 0.35 s before they hit. Read the cue early and move early.
3. **Do the job that lands first.** When two things are up (a tower and a mark, a ring and a star), stand for the one that lands first, then move for the next.
4. **Walk around him, never into him.** His body blocks you; a straight line across the centre gets you stuck.
5. **Two clocks.** Last Light (below 20 %) fills the floor and makes him stronger every slice; Final Noon at 6:30 is the hard stop. Punish every open chest and save cooldowns for Last Light.

## Every ability

### Light hits: dodge the shape

| Ability | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Meridian Lances** | Red beams through the whole arena and through him, aimed at a player | They track for about 1 s, then lock. Side-step ~1.5 m. Near him the beams meet, so step **outward** to where the gaps are wider. |
| **Cascading Lances** (Phase 3) | Two volleys; the second is turned 22.5° | Clear the first, then the second. |
| **Starfall Mirror** | A red circle follows you; a copy lands mirrored through the centre | Step off your star when it locks. Never stand on anyone's mirror. Co-op: don't stand opposite a partner through the centre. |
| **Hour Hands** (every level, Phase 2+) | A gold clock hand per player sweeps out from him | Step off your own hand. |

### Heavy hits: be in the right place

| Ability | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Ecliptic Order** | 4 brass rings numbered I–IV for **2.0 s**, then dark | **Memorise the order.** The floor is struck everywhere except ring I (at 3.2 s), then II, III, IV, 2.2 s apart. Go **around** him between rings, not through him. When paired, the Lances land just before ring II: side-step inside the ring. |
| **Zenith Collapse** | He walks to the centre; everything except a disc at his feet erupts | Get in the disc. **Solar Hunger:** the disc shrinks 0.5 m and the hit grows +5 every cast, to 60 (78 on Hard). When paired with Lances, the beams land 1.2 s after the Collapse: once it lands, step out to about 5 m. |
| **Zenith Marks** | **SUN** or **SHADE** over your head for 3 s | **SUN:** beyond 8.5 m from him. **SHADE:** within 5.5 m. "SHADE > SUN" is two-step: close for the first volley, far for the second. With towers, the mark lands 1.5 s after the last tower, so finish your tower, then go. |
| **Orrery Closing** | A ring at the rim and a crown around him | Stand in the band between. The rim **stays lit** (the Horizon): don't step outside it. Hour Hands comes during it. |
| **Sunspots** | "SUNSPOT I II" over a carrier | Walk to the **edge** (10 m+ from him) before it goes off. Each blast is a heavy hit in a 3.5 m circle and leaves a **burning pool for 75 s**, which carries across phases. **Solo:** let I go, walk 4 m along the edge, let II go. **Co-op:** carriers stay 4.5 m apart; catching another carrier is a **Solar Flare, a wipe**. |

### Fight-enders: mechanics that wipe the party

| Ability | Wipe if… | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|
| **Meridian Towers** | a tower is **short of players** when it lands | Each tower shows **dots: exactly that many players must be inside it at the moment it lands** (it rises 5.0 s early). **No damage when done right.** Extra players in a tower take a **heavy hit**. **Solo:** three towers of one, 2.5 s apart (I → II → III): be in each as it lands, then run to the next. **Co-op:** towers land together and their dots add up to the party (4 players: 2+2 or 1+3). Assigned when they rise in party order; stay out of towers that aren't yours. |
| **Total Eclipse** (intermission at 70 %) | his **Corona Shield** (gold number on his bar) isn't broken in **25 s** | The shield is **700 per fighting player** (700 solo, 2,800 for 4), so it takes the whole party about 22 s. Stay inside the **blue light wells** (3.5 m, orbiting slowly) and attack non-stop. Out of a well for over 2 s, you burn 10 a second. |
| **Severance** (Phase 3) | co-op: one half has nobody in it | A gold line appears and the game calls **"SPLIT"**. You have 3 s: odd players go Day, even players go Night. Then it locks and **sweeps fast: 135° one way in 10 s, then "THE LINE TURNS BACK" (1.5 s warning) and 135° back** (about 3.3 m/s at the rim). Crossing it, or letting it sweep over you, is a heavy hit. Run ahead of it, and be ready to reverse. Co-op ends in the **Reunion** tower (4.5 m at the centre): everyone in. **Solo:** no side is assigned and there is no Reunion, but crossings still hit. |
| **Last Light** (Phase 4, below 20 %) | the 8th slice falls (88 s into Phase 4) | **The soft enrage.** A 45° slice burns for good every **12 s** (every **10 s** from the 5th), with a 3 s warning, in a fixed order: 0°, 180°, 90°, 270°, then the diagonals. **Every fallen slice makes all his attacks 10 % stronger** ("THE LIGHT WANES"). Kill him before the room is gone. |

## Phase by phase

### Phase 1: Perfect Order (100–70 %), hard from the pull

Order:
1. **Ecliptic + Lances**
2. **Towers + Starfall**
3. **Sunspots + Starfall**
4. **Collapse + Lances**
5. **Towers + Marks**
6. **two-step Marks + Lances**
7. punish

- The first tower set comes with only Starfall: learn the tower route there.
- Drop your Sunspots at the edge. Their pools last into Phase 2, so keep the middle clean for later Collapses.

### Intermission: Total Eclipse (at 70 %)

- Into a well at once, and attack non-stop.
- Dodge beams and stars **inside** the well.
- Breaking the shield ends it early.

### Phase 2: Misalignment (70–40 %), three at once

Order:
1. Sunspots + Starfall + Lances
2. Towers + Marks + Starfall
3. Ecliptic + Lances (+ Starfall solo)
4. Collapse + Lances
5. Orrery + Horizon, then Starfall, then Hour Hands
6. punish

- Hold the job that lands first; dodge stars after your tower or ring.

### Phase 3: Exposed Meridian (40–20 %), the second form

Order:
1. **Severance** (3 s split, then a 20 s sweep that turns back at 10 s; Reunion in co-op)
2. Cascading Lances + Starfall + Hour Hands
3. Sunspots + Collapse
4. Towers + Lances + Starfall
5. two-step Marks + Lances
6. punish

- **Sunspots + Collapse:** drop at the edge. The Collapse lands 2.5 s after the last blast, which is enough time to run back to his feet.

### Phase 4: Last Light (20–0 %), the soft enrage

- Lances + Starfall › SUN/SHADE + Lances › Hour Hands + Starfall, on loop, while the floor falls.
- No punish window. Burn him down.
- Drift toward the diagonals; they burn last.

## Playing solo

Everything above applies; these are the solo differences.

| | Solo |
|---|---|
| His HP | 10,000 |
| Towers | three towers of one, landing 5.0, 7.5 and 10.0 s after they rise (Easy 6, 9 and 12 s) |
| Corona Shield | 700 in 25 s |
| Severance | no side is called and there is no Reunion; crossing the line still hits |
| Last Light | starts at 20 %; the 8th slice falls 88 s in, Final Noon at 6:30 |

**Melee (Vanguard):**
- Every swing roots you. Don't start a Sunder or Last Word when a shape is about to land on you; a basic is the safe filler.
- Eclipse: attack from the **inner edge** of a well, the side nearest him, and drift with it as it turns.
- Ecliptic Order: stand on the **inner edge** of the safe ring so he is in reach.
- Towers: keep swinging and leave late; walk around him, not through him.
- Sunspots: you lose damage walking to the edge. Drop them and come straight back.

**Ranged (Chimesinger, Arcanist):** cast from inside the wells and the safe rings, and keep your basic going while you follow a turning well (it casts on the move). You can keep casting while you wait for a tower, so stand in it early.

**Reference clears (perfect solo bots, Unity):**

| | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vanguard | 3:44, Corona in 21.1 s | 4:38, 19.3 s | 4:35, 18.3 s |
| Chimesinger | 3:40, 16.7 s | 3:34, 18.3 s | 3:26, 18.1 s |
| Arcanist | 2:18, 12.3 s | 2:40, 11.2 s | 2:34, 11.2 s |

All of them ended with 1 to 4 slices fallen in Last Light, well before the 8th.


## Potions and damage

- A perfect group still takes some damage: a few Starfalls and Lances, pool ticks, and in Last Light every hit grows 10 % per fallen slice.
- **Drink in the punish windows** (3.5 s, "PUNISH WINDOW": his chest is open and nothing is coming), or in the 2.0 s numeral reveal of Ecliptic Order.
- Drink below 70 HP on Hard; a heavy hit is 65.

## Co-op roles (4 players)

| Job | Rule |
|---|---|
| Towers | Dots tell you how many: towers land together, assigned in party order (4 players: 1–2 → tower 1, 3–4 → tower 2, or 1 → tower 1, 2–4 → tower 2). Never stand in a tower that isn't yours. |
| Severance | Players 1 and 3 Day, 2 and 4 Night (the game calls it). Both pairs meet in Reunion. |
| Sunspot carriers | Rotate automatically; walk to the edge, 4.5 m apart. |
| Corona Shield | Everyone attacks from the wells; use the biggest burst here. |

## Common wipes and their fix

| Wipe | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Total Eclipse — tower II was short" | Be inside as it lands, then run; co-op, get the dot count right and stay out of other towers. |
| "Solar Flare" | Sunspot carriers stood within 3.5 m of each other: spread at the edge. |
| "Total Eclipse — the Corona Shield held" | Attack from inside the wells; don't stop to reposition. |
| "Severance — the Day half fell" | Split during the 3 s warning, as called. |
| "Final Noon" | Too slow: punish every open chest; save cooldowns for the shield and Last Light. |
