What’s new in this demo

October 28, 2026

The Arcanist is finished, and the Tempo Tyrant is much harder (see Also new). The Arcanist plays around glyphs. Channel Disintegrate on the boss to carve them, spend them on full-power Rune Sigils and Arcane Comets, or cash them all into Arcane Surge. Magic Missile fills the gaps. It is still a ranged glass cannon with no defensive ability, so keep moving.

How glyphs work

  • Carve: each damage step of Disintegrate that hits the boss carves one glyph, up to 3. They circle your waist and stay until you spend them.
  • Spend: Rune Sigil and Arcane Comet each spend one glyph for full damage. Cast without one, the rune is drawn faint and deals only 35%.
  • Cash in: Arcane Surge spends every glyph for 2 s of Surge each (up to 6 s). While surging, spells cost no glyph and land twice as fast.

Arcanist abilities

  • Magic MissileLeft click

    Tap: one missile straight at the cursor (10). It can miss.

    Hold: summon up to 3 missiles (5 in Surge); release to fire them straight at the cursor (13 each). They no longer home, so aim.

    You move at half speed while charging. Dashing drops the charge.

  • Disintegrate2 · hold

    A beam you steer toward the cursor for up to 2 s, building in 3 damage steps.

    Each step completed on the boss carves a glyph. You move at 20% speed; dashing breaks the beam but keeps the glyphs already carved. Cooldown 8 s.

  • Rune Sigil3

    Draws a rune at the cursor (16 m) that explodes after 0.8 s (55) on the boss inside it (2.5 m).

    Spends 1 glyph. Without one it is faint and deals 35%. Cooldown 6 s.

  • Arcane Comet4

    Draws a rune at the cursor (20 m); 3 s later a burning meteor crashes into it (90, 4 m). The bullseye in the middle doubles it. The mark stays where you put it, so predict where the boss will be.

    Spends 1 glyph. Without one it deals 35%. Cooldown 18 s.

  • Arcane Surge5

    Spends every glyph (needs at least one) for 2 s of Surge per glyph.

    While surging: charged volleys summon 5 missiles, taps fire two, and Rune Sigil and Arcane Comet cost no glyph and arm or fall twice as fast. A dash during the short cast cancels it and keeps the glyphs. Cooldown 15 s.

  • BlinkRight click

    The shared dash, shown as a teleport. Short burst (3 m in 0.2 s) in the move direction. Costs 30 stamina; sprint and jump share the bar.

    No invincibility: get out of the shape before it resolves.

Plain numbers in brackets, like (10), are damage. Hover over an ability in game to see its tooltip.

Controls & about this demo

Choose your class and difficulty on the main menu, then pick Boss Rush to choose an arena and a boss or miniboss to fight. Start Run (the shuffled run that ends with Bellkeeper) is greyed out in this demo while it is reworked.

Move with WASD, hold Shift to sprint and press Space to jump. Aim with the mouse. Left click for your basic attack (the Arcanist can hold it to charge missiles), 2–5 for class skills, right click to dash, F for a potion and Escape to pause.

Work in progress. Alternative Confrontation is the default main menu. Settings > UI can switch to the five-arena showcase. Boss music continues between fights and crossfades during each countdown. Browser performance may vary.
