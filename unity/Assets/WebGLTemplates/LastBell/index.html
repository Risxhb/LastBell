How to play the Arcanist

The Arcanist plays around glyphs. It is a ranged glass cannon with no defensive ability, so keep moving.

  • Build with Disintegrate: each damage step of the beam that hits the boss carves one glyph, up to 3.
  • Spend on Rune Sigil and Arcane Comet: each spends one glyph for full damage. Without one they deal only 35%.
  • Cash in with Arcane Surge: it spends every glyph for 2 s of Surge each. While surging, spells cost no glyph and land twice as fast.
  • Fill the gaps with Magic Missile while Disintegrate is on cooldown.

Arcanist abilities

  • Magic MissileLeft click

    Filler. Tap: one missile at the cursor (10). Hold: up to 3 missiles (5 in Surge), released at the cursor (13 each). They don't home, so aim. Half speed while charging.

  • Disintegrate2 · hold

    Builder. A beam you steer for up to 2 s, in 3 damage steps; each step on the boss carves a glyph. 20% move speed; dashing breaks the beam but keeps the glyphs. Cooldown 8 s.

  • Rune Sigil3

    Spender (1 glyph). A rune at the cursor (16 m) that explodes after 0.8 s (55, 2.5 m). Cooldown 6 s.

  • Arcane Comet4

    Spender (1 glyph). Mark a spot (20 m); 3 s later a meteor lands there (90, 4 m), doubled in the bullseye. Predict where the boss will be. Cooldown 18 s.

  • Arcane Surge5

    Cash-in (all glyphs, at least 1). 2 s of Surge per glyph: 5-missile volleys, double taps, and free, twice-as-fast Sigils and Comets. Dashing during the cast cancels it and keeps the glyphs. Cooldown 15 s.

  • BlinkRight click

    The shared dash: 3 m in 0.2 s, 30 stamina. No invincibility, so leave the shape before it lands.

Numbers in brackets, like (10), are damage. Hover over an ability in game for its tooltip.

Controls & about this demo

Choose your class and difficulty on the main menu, then pick Boss Rush to choose an arena and a boss or miniboss to fight. Start Run (the shuffled run that ends with Bellkeeper) is greyed out in this demo while it is reworked.

Move with WASD, hold Shift to sprint and press Space to jump. Aim with the mouse. Left click for your basic attack (the Arcanist can hold it to charge missiles), 2–5 for class skills, right click to dash, F for a potion and Escape to pause.

Work in progress. Alternative Confrontation is the default main menu. Settings > UI can switch to the five-arena showcase. Boss music continues between fights and crossfades during each countdown. Browser performance may vary.
