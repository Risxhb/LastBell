# Skyward Spire: image-generation prompt

Written to reproduce `reference-topdown.webp` (the top-down Area 1 reference).

## Main prompt

Top-down orthographic aerial view, looking straight down, of a circular celestial clockwork arena floating high in the open sky. Fantasy game environment concept art in a painterly, highly detailed, semi-realistic style like Hades or Windblown. Bright midday light from the upper left with soft shadows falling to the lower right. Palette: warm ivory and cream stone, polished gold, deep lapis cobalt blue, sky blue and white clouds. Square-ish framing (about 8:7) with the arena centred, filling about 75% of the frame.

**Combat floor (centre).** A large, perfectly flat, open circular plaza of smooth cream-ivory marble. It's laid in wide concentric rings of large curved flagstones with faint hairline seams and very subtle wear. Thin inlaid gold lines trace two concentric circles in the ivory. The inner half of the floor is a huge round dark lapis-lazuli cobalt disc, deep blue with faint mottled stone texture and subtle large tiles. Fine gold inlay on the disc:
- An eight-pointed gold compass star in the exact centre: four long points to north, east, south and west, four shorter diagonal points, each point faceted and split into a light and a shaded half.
- Three thin gold concentric rings around the star, and a thicker gold ring at the disc's edge.
- Eight thin gold radial lines from the star to the disc edge.
- Small gold ring studs (little circles) where the radial lines cross the middle ring, and more small gold rings evenly spaced around the outer part of the disc.

In the ivory ring, at the four cardinal points next to each bridge mouth, sit small gold-framed cobalt-blue diamond (lozenge) emblems. Four smaller gold square or diamond markers sit at the diagonals. The floor has no obstacles, no rubble and no props: it is a clean open combat floor.

**Rim.** The plaza is enclosed by an ornate ivory stone balustrade with a gold-trimmed coping. Dozens of small round ivory pavilion posts are evenly spaced along it, each crowned with a small ribbed gold dome and finial, like a ring of tiny gold-domed turrets. The dais edge drops away into a tall ivory wall with arched gothic openings and gold detailing.

**Bridges.** Four straight ivory stone bridges leave the plaza in a perfect cross (north, east, south and west), each running to the edge of the frame. Each has a smooth cream deck with a thin cobalt inlay line, low ivory railings with gold caps, and a row of tall narrow cobalt-blue banners with gold borders and a gold four-point star emblem on both sides. Small gold-domed posts stand between the banners. Where each bridge meets the plaza there is a slightly wider gatehouse threshold flanked by two gold-domed pavilion posts. The bottom (south) bridge is the entry bridge.

**Ivory belfries (surrounding buildings).** Monumental white-ivory gothic towers rise from the clouds in the four quadrants between the bridges. They're seen from directly above, so their tops are in the foreground and their bases fall away into the depth.
- **Top-left and top-right:** massive cathedral towers with tall pointed-arch windows of cobalt stained glass, each with a gold star, plus flying buttresses, pinnacles, and slender ivory bridges and walkways linking them. Each is crowned with a large ornate gold armillary sphere: interlocking gold rings around a small globe.
- **Left and right middle:** tall round belfries with deep cobalt-blue domes ribbed in gold, gold lanterns and tall gold spire finials. Their sides have tall blue panels with gold star emblems.
- **Lower left:** a round tower with a gold armillary sphere on top, above an open gold-columned bell lantern.
- **Bottom corners:** round ivory towers with gold-domed rotundas.
- Smaller ivory spires, needle pinnacles and gold-capped turrets crowd between the big towers and recede down into the clouds. Gold appears throughout as trims, finials, dome ribs, small orbs and filigree.

**Cloud abyss.** Far below, between the buildings, is a bright sea of fluffy white cumulus clouds with patches of clear sky blue showing through, plus distant smaller towers and spires half-hidden in the cloud. There's a strong sense of height and atmospheric depth: deeper structures are paler and bluer.

**Mood.** Celestial, orderly, radiant, heavenly clockwork citadel. Clean readable silhouettes and crisp gold highlights. The floor stays calm and uncluttered so it reads as a game arena.

## Negative prompt

No characters, no enemies, no text, no labels, no UI, no watermark, no logo, no perspective tilt, no fisheye, no sunset or orange sky, no darkness, no lava, no greenery or vines, no rubble or cracks on the floor, no props on the combat floor, no asymmetric bridges, no extra bridges.

Four straight ivory bridges in a perfect cross, lined with cobalt banners bearing gold stars. Monumental white gothic belfries in each corner with cobalt stained-glass windows, blue gold-ribbed domes and gold armillary spheres on top, plus many ivory spires. A sea of white clouds and blue sky far below, bright midday light from the upper left. No characters, no text.
