Bellkeeper Ultimate v2 · P4 "The Unfinished Hour" example: the Gear Floor (simulation, not the game)

The floor is the clock's gear train: a small gear under the boss and four big gears (N, E, S, W) meshing with it, turning the other way. Between them is the pit. The gears carry you, so you're always walking: away from the spinning hub and the fixed pawl at each gear's outer edge. Every 6 s the hammer slams a random outer gear (it turns coral only 3 s before): be off it, and keep one player on each of the other three or the train jams. After every slam your gear changes, so all four cross through the middle, and the only crossings, the mesh points, bite in turn every 1.5 s. Each slam throws a ring of brass shards (50, two kill). Every third slam hits two gears, and then the gears reverse. Proposal, open to change.

Your job each slam

Over your head: → N/E/S/W (the gear to be on at the next slam) or → CENTRE (your gear is the one being slammed: wait on the middle gear).

The rule: your gear moves two places round each slam, so most slams you cross to the opposite gear, through the centre.

Kills

Standing on the slammed gear: 999. An outer gear left empty at a slam: WIPE (the train jams).

Cores: each outer gear's hub. Hit it and the damage goes to the boss's bar; melee parks against it, where the floor barely moves.

The pit between gears or a pawl: 999. Gears only touch at the mesh points next to the centre gear.

Mistake buttons

Restart with one bot making one mistake.
