Bellkeeper Ultimate v2 · P2 "Broken Hour" example (simulation, not the game)

4 perfect bots. Every 3 s the P1 hand ticks to the next plate and three rules resolve on the same tick: the plate (the next hour soaks it), the Inverted Bell (the bell slams the half away from the plate: everyone must be on the plate's half, except the INVERTED player, who must be inside the slam) and the Gear Train (I–III and II–IV are chained: each chain 6–10 m, and the chains may not cross). INVERTED is always the soaker's partner, so every beat it must be passed by touch to the next one. The P1 rewinds return. Proposal, open to change.

Read each beat

SOAK: the lit plate (hours take turns I, II, III, IV).

INVERTED: stand inside the slam (the far half), and keep your chain 6–10 m from your partner on the plate.

SAFE: stay on the plate's half, chain 6–10 m, don't cross the other chain.

PASS: you hold INVERTED now and must touch the next holder before the beat (it jumps to whoever you touch first, then can't move again for 1 s).

Fails

Plate empty, a chain over 10 m, or the wrong INVERTED holder: WIPE. Wrong half: 999. Chain under 6 m or chains crossed: 60.
