Bellkeeper Ultimate v2 · P3 "The Open Hour" example (simulation, not the game)

Bullet hell. 4 perfect bots weave through the Mainspring (three spiral arms of brass bullets that reverse every 12 s), survive the Rewound Shot (every 15 s every bullet flies back along its own path), never meet their own Echo (where you stood 2 s ago bursts: 999 to you, and its four shots hit anyone), cross each Bell Curtain through the gap with their own numeral, and keep the plates soaked every 6 s. Bullets 50 (two kill), a burst or a wrong gap 999, an empty plate wipes. Proposal, open to change.

What you see

Brass bullets: the Mainspring arms. Ride the gaps between arms; they reverse direction every 12 s.

REWIND: a warning 1.5 s before. Then every bullet turns round and flies back into the boss at 1.5x speed: the gap you came through is a wall again, and next to the boss is the worst place to be.

Echoes: a ring under where each player stood 2 s ago, filling up until it bursts: the burst kills its owner, and its four small shots hit anyone. Keep moving; never double back.

Bell Curtain: a wall sweeping the whole arena with four gaps marked I–IV. Pass through your numeral; another's gap is 999.

Plate: every 6 s the next hour must be on the lit plate.

Mistake buttons

Restart with one bot making one mistake.
